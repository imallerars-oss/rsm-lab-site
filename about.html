Built around a simple problem

Standard ear products don't fit everyone the same way. RSM LAB was built to solve that — with a digital manufacturing process that starts with real ear data and ends with a product made for one person, produced at scale, on repeat.

We work with companies, not individual consumers. That means our process is built for consistency across an order of ten products, or ten thousand.
